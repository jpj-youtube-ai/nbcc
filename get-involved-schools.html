Refer your pupils

Each school refers its own pupils, through its own account. We never ask for a pupil’s name, and collection costs nothing.

Get your school involved

  • A jumper or dress down dayWith your own fundraising page and poster.
  • Be a drop-off pointCollect donations at school, and we come and pick them up.
  • Visit the WorkshopBring your class to see where the magic happens, by arrangement.
  • A talk at assemblyOne of our elves could come and tell your pupils about NBCC.
  • Pupil volunteeringPupils aged 16 or over can volunteer with us, including for the Duke of Edinburgh’s Award.
