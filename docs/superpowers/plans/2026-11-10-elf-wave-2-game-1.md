# Elf Detective School wave 2, Plan 2a: the game and the family screens Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** From the first clue to the scrapbook: a door runs its evening by itself (three clues, typed guesses, the word on the tag, one prize per family, the place shown at its time), every later visit by the word earns stamps and puts the family's elves on the door, a family can send a photo and take it down, head elves get the December's doors switch, Snow day, Gone home, clue pictures and the practice door, and a practice evening proves the whole thing end to end in CI. Everything ships behind the switch, off.

**Architecture:** One migration adds the game tables (`elf_guesses`, `elf_word_tries`, `elf_visits`, `elf_photos`, `elf_photo_files`, `elf_practice`) and the door's found, snow day and gone home columns. The rules stay pure in `src/elf/` (`doors.ts` learns the three clue times, the reveal and the stored snow day; `claim.ts` gains `decideWord`, which is both the claim and the stamp; `guess.ts` becomes typed). A new `src/db/elf-game.ts` reads and writes the game; one loader, `runsFor`, serves every family route and gives the wave 1 doors while the switch is off; `src/elf/views.ts` turns rows into views; the screens in `src/elf/pages/` draw them; `src/routes/elf.ts` gains the `doorsOn` gate, a public calendar built once every 15 seconds, the poster page and the family's API. The practice door is year 0, door 0 in the same tables, seen only by a browser holding the preview cookie, so its absolute clue times let CI play an evening. Plan 2b (`2026-11-10-elf-wave-2-game-2.md`) adds the photo check, alerts, emails, the worker and Finds on top of this; the texts plan (`2026-11-10-elf-wave-2-texts.md`) ships before this one.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript on Express, Postgres with node-pg-migrate, zod, sharp (through `processPicture`), Vitest (jsdom for the scripts), Cucumber BDD (CI only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-elf-detective-wave-2-design.md` (binding), with the secret places design's "Rules, precisely" (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-elf-detective-secret-places-design.md`) for clues, reveal and guesses, and October's "Rules, precisely" (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-elf-detective-school-design.md`) for the word, the six tries and progress. This plan covers decisions 1, 2, 3, 11, calls C3, C4 (the family half and the start), C6, C8 (the family's take down), C11, C12 and the family side of decision 8. The mockup to match is `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-10-elf-wave-2/mockups/door-scrapbook-v2.html` (not in git), with decision 11's two rows in place of its mixed collage. **Plan 1 (`2026-11-10-elf-wave-2-doors.md`) and the texts plan merge first**: this plan reuses `DoorSetup`, `emptyDoor`, `eveningFor`, `readinessOf`, `doorPicture`, `normalisePlaceName`, `sameName`, `makeTagWord`, `makePosterCode`, the `elf_doors`, `elf_door_names` and `elf_clues` tables, `src/db/elf-doors.ts`, `src/routes/admin-elf-doors.ts` and the Doors tile.

Two rulings from the coordinator, recorded here because they change the mockup and an earlier reading of the spec: **the photo row is in a stable shuffled order** (sorted by each photo's random token), not approval order, because approval order lets a reader guess which photo belongs to the finder named above it (decision 11's stated purpose is that nobody can match a face to a name); and **the prize is named on every door once its elf is found**, whether or not Show it off is ticked.

  - `interface DoorFacts { clue1At: Date; clue2At: Date; clue3At: Date; revealAt: Date; closesAt: Date; foundAt: Date | null; snowDay: boolean; goneHome: boolean }`. `revealAt` is the staff time the place is shown; `closesAt` is `closingInstant`'s answer; `snowDay` is true when `elf_doors.snow_day_at` is set (by a head elf, or automatically when clue 1 fell due on a door that could not run: Task 4).
