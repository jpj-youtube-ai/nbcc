A few simple rules

  • Please don’t stretch, recolour or redraw the logo. Resize it by its corner so it keeps its shape.
  • Leave space around it, at least the width of the elf’s hat bobble, so it can breathe.
  • Use the white logo on dark backgrounds, and the colour logo on light ones.
  • Say you are fundraising for NBCC, for example “Sam’s Santa Dash, fundraising for NBCC”. You are not fundraising on behalf of NBCC or as part of our team.
  • Not sure? Send it to us before it goes out. We are always happy to take a look.
