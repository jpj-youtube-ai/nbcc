# Elf Detective School, Plan 2c-2: Christmas Eve, the grand prize and the Graduate's certificate Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On Christmas Eve every detective who has done all 24 doors can send in one picture for the grand prize from Detective HQ until the entries close (5pm by default, a Head Elf HQ setting that can never be earlier than an hour after door 24's place closes); head elves judge the pictures in a new Grand prize part and mark one winner; in the week before, the clue email asks families who are up to date whether their picture is ready and tells the rest what they can still crack from home, and 23 December's email says Christmas Eve starts in the morning; graduates get their certificate, share card and video link; families can stop the emails that are not essential; and Doors names the head elf on its ticks. Everything ships behind December's doors switch, off.

**Architecture:** One migration adds the entries (`elf_grand_prize_entries`, with their pictures in `elf_grand_prize_files`, kept out of the backups as photos are), two settings (`grand_prize_closes_at`, `graduate_video_url`) and the families' email switch (`emails_stopped_at`, `emails_token`). The rules are pure in a new `src/elf/grand-prize.ts` and the store is a new `src/db/elf-grand-prize.ts`. The family's side is in `src/routes/elf.ts` and Detective HQ: a grand prize section from Christmas Eve, an entry page with a small script that shrinks the picture as the photo sheet does, the certificate and the share card. Head Elf HQ's side is a new `src/routes/admin-elf-prize.ts`, a Grand prize tab and a Settings panel in `assets/js/admin/elf-hq.js`. The worker adds the week before's lines, Christmas Eve's line and the stop link to the clue email.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript on Express, Postgres with node-pg-migrate, zod, sharp (through `processPicture`, and for the share card), Vitest (jsdom for the scripts), Cucumber BDD (CI only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-elf-catch-up-and-grand-prize-design.md` (approved by Jaimie on 12 November 2026; its answers and "my calls" are binding): part 2 in full, the three carried items still open (Stop these emails, the head elf named on Doors' ticks; the photo route is Plan 2c-1's), and answers 1, 3 and 4. The October design (`2026-10-04-elf-detective-school-design.md`, decisions 8 and 16) describes the certificate, the share card and the video link.

**Starts from Plan 2c-1** (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-12-elf-catch-up.md`) as built and merged: `FamilyKnowledge` and `familyKnowledge`, `doorsToCrack`, `newYearsDay`, `closingShown` (`src/elf/catch-up.ts`, `src/db/elf-game.ts`), `DoorsDone.family` and `doneDoorsOf` (`src/elf/progress.ts`), the clue email's `closesAt` and its new lines, `knowledgeFor` in `src/routes/elf.ts`, and the migration `1791200000470_elf-catch-up.js`. ## Planner's calls

Where the spec is silent, decided here:

1. The entries' closing time is one time on Christmas Eve, set in Head Elf HQ > Settings by any head elf with elf edit (the spec: "a time a head elf sets"). It is stored as a moment, `elf_settings.grand_prize_closes_at`; empty means 5pm on 24 December of Elf Detective School's year (`elf_settings.year`).
2. "An hour after door 24's place closes" is measured from the latest door 24 can open: the later of its reveal time and its closing time, which is 8pm while no closing time is typed. So with door 24's closing time not typed, 5pm cannot be saved, and both Settings and door 24 in Doors warn until it is typed, by 4pm.
3. Entries open at midnight starting 24 December, London time; in practice nobody finishes before door 24's first clue.
4. One picture a detective, replaced in place until the entries close. The family can see its own picture (`private, no-store`). Sending needs the picture script, as photos do, and shares the photo's daily tries limit (20 a family).
5. A mobile typed with an entry goes on the family (the join form's column, words and check), and only when the family has none.
6. The winner can be marked only once the entries have closed, so a picture never changes under the judge. One winner a year is kept by a unique index; marking another moves it.
7. Entry pictures are kept out of the nightly backups, as photos are (carried item 6: "the same handling as photos"), and are left to the January clear out, still to be built, which must take them; the README says so.
8. The share card is a PNG made on the server from `shareCardSvg` (the October plan's "Stage B"). The runtime image gains `fonts-dejavu-core`, because a picture drawn on the server can only use fonts the server has.
9. The graduates' video link is a Head Elf HQ setting (an https link, or none), so no developer is needed to add it; with none, Detective HQ shows no video link.
10. The certificate is dated the day it is printed (a detective can only graduate from Christmas Eve, so it is never earlier).
11. Stop these emails: every family gets a random token from the migration; the link in an email opens a page with a button, because following a link never changes anything; stopping and starting are audited. Families get no reminder email today, so "the reminders" are the lines that ride in the clue email, and stopping the clue email stops them. A stopped family's promotions are claimed and marked without an email, so starting again never sends old news. The won email always goes.
12. The week before's lines and Christmas Eve's line ride in the clue email, so a snowed in door 23 sends neither. A family that is behind with nothing left to crack from home is told to visit the places it has not done.
13. Detective HQ's grand prize section shows from midnight starting 24 December until the later of the closing time and 1 January.
14. Pictures for Jaimie (the spec's "show them to Jaimie as pictures first"): the entry page, Detective HQ's grand prize section and Head Elf HQ's Grand prize part are rendered on pretend data after their tasks' review and shown to Jaimie before the pull request merges, switched off; a change she asks for is made in the same pull request.
