New to referrals? Here’s how it works

Every bag that reaches the right person is a Christmas morning that matters.

We’re here all year: refer for a Red Bag Full of Joy at Christmas, and for school clothing and crisis support whenever it’s needed.

Schools and nurseries each refer their own pupils, through their own account. If you work with a child at school or nursery, please refer through them.

Other organisations who support a baby not yet at nursery, a young person who has left school, or a vulnerable adult can ask to become a referrer. It takes about two minutes.

Three steps

  1. They are referred to us, by their school, nursery or a service we work with.
  2. Volunteers pack the bag, through November and December, chosen for that person.
  3. It arrives before Christmas, delivered to the school or to the door, in time for Christmas morning.

For schools and nurseries, collection costs nothing. If you’d like your bags delivered, we ask for one £30 contribution per delivery, however many bags there are.

Why we built a new referral system

Everyone who refers now agrees to clear rules first, so every Red Bag Full of Joy reaches someone who truly needs it.

We’re not an emergency service. If somebody is at risk right now, please contact social work or the police.

Need a little help yourself? Speak to your school, health visitor or support worker, and they can refer you to us.

Any questions? Ring us on 01292 811 015, or email referrals@nbcc.scot. A real person reads it.
