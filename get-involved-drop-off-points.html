New to drop-off points? Here’s how it works

Drop-off points are local places, like village halls, churches, shops, offices and schools, where people leave donations for us to collect. Every one helps pack a Red Bag Full of Joy.

Who can be one? Shops, cafes, pubs and restaurants, offices, churches and community halls, schools, colleges and nurseries, gyms and leisure centres. Even a box or two by the door helps.

If you’d like to be one

Every drop-off point must sign up, even if you’ve helped us before.

  1. Complete the sign up. It takes about five minutes.
  2. One of our team looks it over and may give you a ring. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
  3. If it’s a yes, we email you a link to set up your account. From there you can see when we’re coming to collect, and tell us when donations are ready.
  4. If you’d like, your venue goes on our public list so people can find you.
