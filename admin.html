Gift Aid claims

NBCC reclaims 25p of tax from HMRC for every £1 given by a UK taxpayer who made a Gift Aid declaration. You file the claim through HMRC’s Charities Online service. This page prepares the claim and tracks it; it does not file it for you.

How a Gift Aid claim works, step by step
  1. One-time setup (HMRC). Enrol for Charities Online in NBCC’s HMRC online account (choose “Charities”). HMRC posts an activation code; it arrives in about a week and must be used within 28 days.
  2. Build a batch here. In 1. Ready to claim below, tick the Gift-Aided donations you want to claim and add them to a batch. One batch is one HMRC claim (up to 1,000 donations; above that HMRC needs claiming software).
  3. Export the batch. Under 2. Claim batches, click Export CSV. The file lists each donation with the donor’s name, house name/number, postcode, date and amount: exactly the columns HMRC’s schedule needs.
  4. Fill HMRC’s schedule spreadsheet. Download HMRC’s official Gift Aid schedule spreadsheet (an .ods file) and copy the exported rows into it. HMRC only accepts their spreadsheet, so don’t rename its worksheet tab or it won’t attach.
  5. Submit on Charities Online. Log in to HMRC, attach the filled schedule to the online claim form, and send it.
  6. Mark the batch submitted. Come back here and click Submit on the batch, so NBCC’s records match what you sent HMRC.
  7. HMRC pays. Repayment arrives by BACS, normally within about 4 weeks (HMRC target: 20 working days).
  8. Keep the records. Keep each donor’s declaration for 6 years. NBCC stores these automatically so HMRC can audit them.

Refunded a donation you had already claimed? See 3. Adjustment due. Enter that amount in the adjustment box on your next Charities Online claim, which repays HMRC by reducing the new claim. Deadline: claim within 4 years of the end of the tax year the donation was received. This is a plain-English summary of HMRC guidance, not tax advice.

GASDS deadline

GASDS (the Gift Aid Small Donations Scheme) tops up small cash and contactless donations, but the claim window is only two years from the end of the tax year of collection. These small donations are approaching or past that cliff and are not yet marked as claimed. Tick the ones you have claimed under GASDS to clear them.

        Preview password

        What you give trustees and the sponsor so they can see the page before it's public. Changing it signs out anyone using the old one, which is usually the point. You never need to touch AWS for this.

        Capacity and sales

        Seats held back never go on sale — use it for comps, the sponsor’s guests and the top table. Individual tickets share tables, so a table only stays sellable while nobody has been seated at it.

        Seats held back

        Take seats or tables off sale for someone by name — a company waiting on an invoice, the sponsor’s guests, the top table. Give it a date and the seats go back on sale by themselves if nothing comes of it, so a forgotten hold cannot quietly shrink the room.

        Leave “hold until” empty to hold the seats until someone releases them by hand.

        Card fee

        What Stripe charges NBCC. The ticket page offers buyers the chance to cover it, so this must be the rate you are actually on — check it against a real payment in the Stripe dashboard, not a price list. Stripe charges this once per booking, not per ticket.

        Details the venue confirms later

        Leave these empty and the page says “to be confirmed” rather than guessing. Fill one in and it appears on the ticket page and in every booking email from then on.

        The week-before reminder

        One email to everyone who has paid: the practical details, plus a read-back of the guests and dietary notes they gave us, so a mistake gets caught while there is still time to fix it. Anyone already sent one is skipped, so pressing this twice is safe.

        The menu

        Paste what the venue sends, one course per line, options separated by a vertical bar. A line with no options is a course everybody gets. Leave this empty and guests see no menu section at all — which is how it ships, because the menu is not confirmed yet.

        When guest details close

        The date the venue needs final numbers by. Nothing is chased until you set this — an email asking for names with no date in it is just nagging, and it spends the one message people actually read. Once it is set, anyone still outstanding gets a reminder a fortnight before, and a last call on the day itself.

        Guest details still to come

        Guest names, dietary requirements and access needs come from the person who booked, through the link in their confirmation email. Most of a table of ten arrives because the buyer chased nine other people, so this is who has not replied yet — biggest gap first. Their link opens the same page they were emailed, so you can resend it rather than asking them to find an old email.
