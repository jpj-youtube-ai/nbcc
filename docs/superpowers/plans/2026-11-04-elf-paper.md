# Elf Detective School in paper Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every wave 1 Elf Detective School page in the approved paper cut look, and the dress up wardrobe rebuilt from whole costumes and extras with a per door unlock rule, ready to go live switched off.

**Architecture:** The approved paper pictures are copied into `assets/img/elf/paper/` and the torn paper shapes are drawn once into small SVG files. A new catalogue (costumes, scenes, friends, frames, glasses, bow ties) is added to the shared `assets/js/elf-wardrobe.js` beside the old one, then every reader of a look switches to it at once and the old layers are deleted. The paper look is one set of `.elf-` classes in `assets/css/elf.css` plus server drawn SVG pieces in a new `src/elf/pages/paper.ts`, applied page by page.

**Tech Stack:** Express 4, TypeScript, plain browser JavaScript (no build step), hand written CSS, sharp (already a dependency), Postgres via node-pg-migrate, Vitest with jsdom, Cucumber BDD (CI only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-elf-paper-design.md` (approved by Jaimie on 4 November 2026, decisions 1 to 13). It amends `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-elf-painted-wardrobe-design.md`, decisions 14 and 15 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-elf-detective-school-design.md`, and lines 33 to 34 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-elf-detective-secret-places-design.md`; it wins where they disagree. The wave 1 plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-elf-detective-wave1-sign-up.md` is built; this plan continues on its branch.

**Not in this plan:** wave 2 (door tables, clues and guesses, the places, the poster and print routes, the photo at the place); restyling the emails or the admin screens (only the welcome email's words change).
