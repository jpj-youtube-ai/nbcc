# NBCC Elf Detective School: secret places and the clue ladder

A change to `2026-10-04-elf-detective-school-design.md` (the main design). Where the two disagree,
this one wins. Everything the main design says that this does not touch still stands.

Agreed with Jaimie on 3 November 2026, after the clickable walkthrough.

## Why

As first designed, the hunt was a race to places everyone already knew. The list of the 24 hiding
places was public from launch, a public map was pencilled in, every shop's poster went up on
1 December and stayed all month, and the daily guess was a pick from that list. Families could work
out the shops in advance and stand outside one waiting for the elf to come out at 5:30pm.

Jaimie (verbatim): "I don't think we should be telling them the location of the place. Otherwise,
you might end up with people queuing outside those places just waiting to go in as they open, or
waiting for the 5:30 for someone to put out the elf. ... it needs to be way more of a guess the
actual place, guess the actual shop or the thing."

So the hunt becomes a real puzzle: each day a ladder of clues, getting more obvious, and the only
way to know where an elf is hiding is to work it out.

## Goals

- Nobody can know a place before its elf is already out.
- Each day's clues start hard and get more obvious, so younger children and far away families get
  there in the end, and the sharpest detectives get there first.
- Only families who have signed up and paid see the clues.
- No crowd has a reason to gather anywhere before a clue.

## Non-goals

- No change to claiming, the secret word, candy cane stamps, ranks, outfits, the wardrobe or the
  certificate.
- No map pin guessing, no picking a town from a list (both considered and turned down).
- No gold, silver or bronze for guessing early.

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)

- Any public page, email to the public, poster, map or social post that lets someone list this
  year's hiding places, or tie a place to a day, before that day's elf is out.
- A clue on any page that can be read without signing in. (The clue 1 email can be forwarded; Jaimie
  chose to email it, and that is accepted.)
- A prize given at random to people who paid to enter (a lottery; under 16s may not take part).

## Decisions (Jaimie's, 3 November 2026)

1. **Launch date.** Sign ups open on **Friday 13 November 2026** ("not this Friday, but next
   Friday"), not Sunday 8 November. School starts on Tuesday 1 December as before.

2. **The places are secret.** There is no public list of the hiding places and no public map of
   them (main design decisions 3 and 20 are dropped on this point). A place is shown only on its own
   door, once that door is open (found, or revealed at closing time), as the main design already
   says. The admin's planning map stays, for staff only.

3. **A clue ladder each day.** Each door has three clues, from hard to nearly a giveaway. Staff
   write them in the admin. Each clue can be words, a picture, or both. Clue ideas for staff (help
   text in the admin, not rules):
   - Hard: a riddle about what the place does; an extreme close up photo; a picture puzzle; a few
     lines from the elf's diary about what it can hear and smell.
   - Medium: which town (a riddle or a nearby landmark); a square on a grid map of the area; the
     view out of the shop window.
   - Easy: the first letter of each word and how many letters ("C _ _ _ _ _ B _ _ _ _ _ _ _ _ in
     Girvan"); the street; the shop sign, a little blurred.

4. **One evening, starting when the elf goes out.** Clue 1 comes out at the door's clue time, which
   is when the elf and the poster go out. Clue 2 follows 30 minutes later and clue 3 30 minutes
   after that. Defaults: 5:30pm, 6pm, 6:30pm on school days; 10am, 10:30am, 11am at weekends and on
   school holidays. Staff can change each of the three times for any door in the admin. Clue 1's
   time is the door's clue time in every existing rule (the door goes from `shut` to `hunting` then).

5. **Only signed up families see the clues.** A clue appears in Detective HQ, behind the sign in,
   at its time. The public calendar shows the doors, their countdowns and the open doors' places,
   but never a clue's words or picture: before clue 1 it counts down to it; after, it says the clue
   is out and to sign in to Detective HQ to see it.

6. **One clue email a day.** At clue 1's time each family gets one email with clue 1, which says when
   clues 2 and 3 land in Detective HQ, with a button to it. Clues 2 and 3 are not emailed. Because
   emails go out at about 60 a minute, Detective HQ shows clue 1 at its time whether or not a
   family's email has arrived yet, and the email says so ("If you are reading this a little late,
   the clue has been in Detective HQ since 5:30pm").

7. **The guess is typed.** With no list to pick from, a family types where they think the elf is.
   One guess per family per door, saved and changeable while the door is `hunting`, as now. Each
   place has its name and a short list of other names that also count, set by staff in the admin
   (for example "Corner Bakehouse", "The Bakehouse", "Bakehouse Girvan"). Nothing says whether a
   guess is right until the door opens, as now. A guess cracks the door when, at the moment the door
   opens, the saved guess matches one of that place's names and was saved before it opened.

8. **Magnifying glasses earn elf things only.** A cracked door counts as done, towards the next
   present, outfits and promotions, exactly as now. Nothing more.

9. **The main prize stays judged.** As in the main design: Jodie, Head Elf (or the judge NBCC
   chooses), picks the winner from the children's drawings or photos on Christmas Eve. Signing up is
   what lets a child into that judging. It is never drawn at random.

10. **Posters go up with the elf.** Each shop puts its QR poster up at the same moment as its elf,
    at clue 1's time, and leaves it up for the rest of December, for candy cane visits once its
    door is open. Before its day the poster is kept out of sight. The staff card and the day's shop
    reminder say so.

11. **An early find does not end the guessing.** If an elf is found before the evening's clues are
    all out, the door says at once who found it and when ("Found by Detective R at 5:47pm!") but
    keeps the place hidden until the door's reveal time: by default 30 minutes after clue 3, so 7pm
    on school days and 11:30am at weekends and school holidays. Clues 2 and 3 still come out on time
    and every family can keep guessing until the place is shown.

12. **Prize carvings stay.** Each door is still carved with its prize's shape, and "Show off" prizes
    are still shown on their doors, as in the main design. Instead, staff choose prizes that do not
    point at the shop giving them (a bike shop's bike behind its own door would). The admin says so
    beside the prize and the "Show off" tick.

13. **Two waves, starting now.** Jaimie lifted the hold on Stage B on 3 November 2026 so that sign
    ups can open on Friday 13 November. Built in its own worktree, apart from the other sessions.
    - Wave 1, live by Friday 13 November: sign up, paying, accounts and sign in, Detective HQ before
      school starts, the wardrobe, the welcome email, and the public calendar counting down.
    - Wave 2, live before Tuesday 1 December: the admin for places, prizes, the three clues, the
      names that also count and the times; the daily clue email; guessing, claiming, stamps and
      everything that runs each day.
    Everything still ships switched off until staff turn it on, and staff approve all public words.

## Rules, precisely (replacing the matching parts of the main design)

- **Clue times.** Each door has `clue1At`, `clue2At`, `clue3At`. Defaults as in decision 4. Each must
  be on the door's own day and later than the one before; the admin refuses otherwise. A clue is
  "out" from its time. The door's existing `clueAt` is `clue1At`.
- **Reveal time.** Each door has `revealAt`, by default 30 minutes after `clue3At`; staff can change
  it, and it must be after `clue3At`.
- **When the place is shown.** A door's place is shown at the later of `revealAt` and whichever
  comes first of the find and the closing time. So an early find waits for `revealAt`; an elf still
  unfound at `revealAt` keeps the hunt going until it is found or the place closes, as now. This
  moment is the door's opening moment (`openedAt`) in every existing rule: guessing is allowed from
  clue 1 until it, a guess cracks the door only if saved before it, candy cane visits count from it,
  and the public calendar shows the place only from it.
- **Found, place still hidden.** Between a find and the place being shown, the public calendar shows
  "Found by Detective R at 5:47pm!" (the finder's public detective name, as now) and no place.
  Claiming is closed then, because the elf has been won.
- **Closing time.** As in the main design, with one more warning: the admin warns when a place closes
  less than an hour after clue 3.
- **Clue content.** Each clue has words (up to about 500 characters), an optional picture with its
  alt text, or both, and at least one of them. A clue is never sent to a browser, an email or a
  cache before its time. Pictures are served only to a signed in family, and only once their clue
  is out.
- **Guess text.** Up to 80 characters. Empty is refused. Matching ignores case, spaces, punctuation,
  accents and a leading "the" (my call), the same way the secret word is compared. The family's
  guess is shown back to them as they typed it.
- **Guessing a place that is already open.** If a family's typed guess matches a place whose door is
  already open, the screen says so kindly ("That elf was already found behind door 3. Try again!").
  That place is public by then, so this gives nothing away (my call).
- **Places already open stay out of play.** A door can only be cracked by its own place, so a guess
  naming another open place never cracks anything, as now.
- **What a guess reveals.** Saving a guess never says whether it is right, never says whether the
  typed place is in this year's hunt, and gets the same answer for any text.
- **A picture in clue 1's email** is carried inside the email itself (an inline attachment), never
  as a link to the website, because an email client loads pictures without signing in (my call).
- **Names added late.** Staff can add a name that also counts for a place until midnight at the end
  of its door's day, so a near miss reported on the night ("we typed Corner Bakery") can be put
  right; guesses are checked against the names as they then stand, which can only give more
  magnifying glasses, never take one away. A name cannot be removed once its door is open. After
  that midnight the results are fixed (my call).
- **Typed guesses are private.** A family's guesses are shown only to that family. They never appear
  on a public page or in an export. Staff see them only as counts (how many cracked a door).

## What changes in what is already built (Stage A, local only)

- `src/elf/guess.ts`: the guess becomes typed text matched against a place's names, replacing
  `guessOptions` and the "pick from the list" rules. Unit tests change with it.
- `src/elf/calendar.ts` and `src/elf/doors.ts`: three clue times and a reveal time per door, with
  the defaults above, and the place shown at the moment set out under "When the place is shown".
  `hunting` still starts at clue 1; the builder decides whether "found, place still hidden" is a new
  door state or a found door whose `openedAt` is later.
- `src/elf/pages/types.ts`, `calendar.ts`, `hq.ts`: the public calendar loses its list of places and
  never carries a clue; Detective HQ shows the clues that are out, with the times of the next ones,
  and a typed guess box in place of the list.
- `src/elf/print.ts`: the staff card says to put the elf and the poster out together at clue 1's
  time and keep the poster out of sight until then. It no longer says the poster stays up all month.
- The walkthrough (a private Artifact) is brought in line: no places list in Detective HQ, three
  clues across the evening, a typed guess, the poster going up with the elf.
