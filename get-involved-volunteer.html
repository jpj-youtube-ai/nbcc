New to volunteering with us? Here’s what it’s like

Every Red Bag Full of Joy is packed, checked and delivered by volunteers.

Pack Red Bags in our workshop. Drive bags out to schools. Walk alongside the Santa Sleigh on a winter evening. Take photos. Help with our social media. Or bring a skill we have not even thought of yet.

Join us in Annbank, or help from home. Regular shifts in November and December, one off events, or all year round. A few hours or more, it all counts.

Never volunteered before? Good.

Neither had some of us. Don’t know anyone? You will.

You need to be 16 or over. You don’t need a PVG to volunteer with us. Doing your Duke of Edinburgh’s Award? Come and do it with us.

Does your employer give you time to volunteer? Many do, and some will match it with a donation. It’s worth asking.

Want to come as a team or group? We’d love that. Groups are by arrangement: see how your business or your club or group can help.

What happens next

  1. Complete the sign up. It takes about five minutes.
  2. A Head Elf reads every application personally. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
  3. If we can take you on, we’ll send you a link to set up your account, and you tell us when you’re free.
